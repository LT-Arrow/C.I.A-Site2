PLAYER SUPPORT

Back to the field.

For playtest help, bug reports or privacy questions, email carlribbeck@gmail.com.

Report a problem.

Include the game version, mission and stage, whether you were hosting or joining, and the steps that caused the issue. Explain what you expected and what happened.

A screenshot or short clip can help. Review it first for private information. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, account tokens or payment details.

Keep your build together.

Fully extract the downloaded ZIP, keep every folder together and launch START_GAME.cmd. Unreal Editor and Python are not needed.

If prompted for the Microsoft Visual C++ runtime, the package includes Microsoft's official redistributable. Read its installer prompts normally.

Field controls.

WASD move · mouse look · Shift sprint · Space jump · E interact · M mission board · B agency office · R aimed stun · H medkit · Q barrier · F flashlight · Escape pause.

Hold V for proximity voice in a connected squad. F10 mutes incoming and outgoing voice. F9 or REC starts/stops a local gameplay clip; F8 opens Videos/Crossworlds.

Connection help.

Can my friend join from another house?

The current public ZIP is LAN-only. Use the matching private EOS build for internet testing. Epic sign-in and hosting have passed, but remote joining still needs verification. Before Epic's application review completes, ordinary external accounts can be rejected.

Why is the room missing?

All players need compatible builds. Use the same LAN or Internet mode. The host must be at HQ without an open case, and a squad has four slots including the host. A full room or deployed squad may be excluded from the room browser.

Where are my clips?

Clips save on your PC under Videos/Crossworlds as AVI files. Stop recording normally so the file can finish. Clips can contain nearby teammates' voices when those voices play through the game mix.

Can I move rooms or customize every part of my character?

Those systems are not finished. Current playtests include four agent roles, agency upgrades and a headquarters hub. Floor three is a Full Game Only preview.
