CROSSWORLDS / EXPEDITIONS

A different descent. Every time.

Six ready-made expeditions. Persistent maps, individual loot and room for your own story. Administrators install and publish these packs in Discord.

How to start an expedition ↗
THE EXPEDITION LOOP

Find the way.
Face what follows.

Select a published dungeon and owned character, prepare a loadout, ready your party, then begin. The leader moves the squad. Grey directions are walls; the gate lists any remaining objectives.

  1. 01
    Explore with /map.Fog keeps undiscovered rooms concealed.
  2. 02
    Choose automatic combat or RP mode.Actions follow actual ability costs and cooldowns.
  3. 03
    Rest where it is safe.HP carries over. Heal at rest services or with owned potions.
  4. 04
    Defeat guardians and reach the gate.Run maps, cleared rooms and earned rewards persist.
