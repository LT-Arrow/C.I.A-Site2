This notice describes the Windows development playtest and this game website.

Who operates this game.

Crossworlds C.I.A. — After Hours is developed under Crossworld devs / LT_Arrow. For support or privacy questions, contact carlribbeck@gmail.com.

Epic account sign-in.

The private internet build uses Epic Account Services and requests Basic Profile permission: Epic account ID, display name, language and linked-account display names. The game does not ask for your Epic password directly; you enter it through Epic's sign-in flow. The current application does not request friends, presence or country access.

Epic processes sign-in, account linking, session-service information and SDK service diagnostics under its own policies. See Epic's privacy policy. Epic authentication may store authentication or cache data through its SDK.

Hosted multiplayer.

A player hosts each squad on their PC. Session discovery processes room information such as host name, player capacity and whether the HQ is open. Connected players and the host exchange gameplay information, including agent identity, positions, health, readiness, mission actions and connection information. Epic and participating hosts may process network identifiers such as IP addresses to establish and operate connections.

This version has no dedicated Crossworlds account server. The hosting player controls the agency save on their PC. Guest field gear and unfinished cases are session-only in this milestone.

Proximity voice.

Holding V in a connected multiplayer squad captures your Windows default microphone and transmits voice through the host to nearby squad members, within an 18-metre gameplay radius. Releasing V, pausing, losing focus, being downed or leaving the pawn stops capture. Solo mode and startup do not open a microphone stream. F10 mutes incoming and outgoing voice.

The voice component does not itself store a voice archive. Other players can hear your transmitted audio, and a player using the recorder can save remote voice that plays through their game audio. Use voice only when you are comfortable with that possibility.

Optional recording.

F9 or REC starts or stops a local AVI clip of the game viewport and mixed game audio, saved to Videos/Crossworlds on that Windows user's PC. F8 opens the folder. The recorder does not capture the desktop or other applications. It does not add the recording player's microphone as sidetone; remote voice audible through the game mix can be included. The recorder does not automatically upload these clips. The player controls whether to share or delete them.

Local saves, settings and diagnostics.

Agency saves, game settings, logs and crash files may be stored on the PC running the game. Logs can include account, session, player and network identifiers. Review files before sharing a bug report. Personal music or images that you add through the local media folders are not automatically published by the game. Local saves, clips and logs remain on the device until removed by its user or the device's file-management tools.

This website and third-party services.

The existing Crossworlds website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Download and repository links use GitHub. Those services may process visitor and download information under their own policies; see the GitHub privacy statement. This notice does not describe the separate Crossworlds Discord bot's server records.

Choices and privacy questions.

You can play the separate solo/LAN build without Epic sign-in, mute voice, leave a hosted session, choose not to record and delete files stored on your own PC. Information controlled by Epic or another player's PC must be addressed with that party. For questions about information held by Crossworld devs, or to request access, correction or deletion of information you have provided to the developer, email carlribbeck@gmail.com. Include enough information to identify your request, and do not include passwords or tokens. Requests about local files on another player's PC must be directed to that player; requests about Epic account data must be directed to Epic. Do not send passwords, authentication codes or access tokens in a support request.

Changes.

We will update this notice when the game's data practices change, including any future dedicated servers, developer analytics, cloud storage or additional account and moderation services.
